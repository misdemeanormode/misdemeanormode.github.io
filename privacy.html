Privacy Policy

MisdemeanorMode Clip Tracker · Last updated: November 19, 2026

MisdemeanorMode Clip Tracker ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used by the MisdemeanorMode TikTok account to see how its own posted clips perform. It is not offered to the public.

What the app reads from TikTok

Only after you sign in with TikTok and approve it, the app reads:

  • your TikTok display name (permission user.info.basic), and
  • your own public videos' ID, caption, posting time, link, and view, like, comment and share counts (permission video.list).

The app does not post, edit, delete or comment on anything, and does not read messages, followers or other people's data.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the computer the app runs on. Video numbers are saved in local files. The TikTok sign-in tokens and app keys are stored in Windows Credential Manager, encrypted for the Windows user. Nothing is uploaded to any server run by us, and we do not sell, share or use the data for advertising.

How it is used

Only to show which of your clips got the most views and engagement, so you can decide what to post next.

Removing access and data

Click Disconnect in the app's Settings to delete the sign-in from the computer. You can also remove the app at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's folder deletes all saved numbers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
